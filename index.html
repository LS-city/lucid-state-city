Welcome to LSC

Walk the city, step into the clubs, and click anything glowing with a sign to see what's there.

Computer: W A S D to walk, drag to look, Shift to run, E or click to open what's in front of you.
Phone: joystick to walk, drag to look, tap a sign.
Chat: press Enter (or the Chat button) and say hi. Your words pop up over your head.
Have fun: 1 wave, 2 dance, 3 cheer, 4 hearts, F fireworks. Press E by a seat to sit, by an arcade machine to play, or at a bar to order a drink.
